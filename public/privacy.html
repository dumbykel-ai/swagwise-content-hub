Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Marlene T LLC ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Swagwise mobile application ("the App"). Swagwise is an AI-powered fashion styling app designed to help professional women build confidence through personalized outfit recommendations.

By using Swagwise, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. We are committed to protecting your privacy and will never sell your personal data to third parties.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly:

  • Account information: name and email address when you create an account
  • Style profile: body type, skin tone, style preferences, and occasion needs you provide during onboarding
  • Wardrobe uploads: photos of clothing items you voluntarily upload for wardrobe analysis
  • Conversations with Stylo: messages and styling requests you submit to our AI stylist

Information collected automatically:

  • Device information: device type, operating system, and unique device identifiers
  • Usage data: features you interact with, outfit recommendations viewed, and app session duration
  • Subscription and payment status: managed securely through Apple's App Store — we do not store payment card details

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide personalized outfit recommendations through our AI stylist, Stylo
  • Generate your style profile and wardrobe analysis
  • Improve the accuracy and relevance of our AI recommendations over time
  • Manage your subscription and account
  • Send you app updates, new features, and relevant styling content (you may opt out at any time)
  • Ensure the security and proper functioning of the App
  • Comply with legal obligations

Important: Swagwise's outfit recommendations are always based solely on what is best for you. We do not accept payments from brands or retailers to influence Stylo's recommendations. Our styling advice is genuinely independent.

3. AI and Data Processing

Swagwise uses artificial intelligence to generate personalized styling advice. Your style profile, wardrobe information, and conversation history with Stylo are used to provide contextually relevant recommendations. AI-generated responses are produced using your provided information combined with our styling knowledge base.

Conversations with Stylo may be reviewed by our team in anonymized form to improve recommendation quality. We will never use your personal identifying information for this purpose without your consent.

4. Wardrobe Photos

If you choose to upload photos of your clothing items, these images are:

  • Stored securely on Cloudflare R2 cloud infrastructure
  • Used solely to provide wardrobe analysis and styling recommendations within the App
  • Never shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes
  • Deletable by you at any time through your account settings

5. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted third-party services that help us operate the App, including cloud storage (Cloudflare R2), AI processing (Anthropic), and analytics. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and may only use it to provide services on our behalf.
  • Legal requirements: If required by law, court order, or governmental authority.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you with the App's services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us at the address below. Upon request, we will delete your data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), secure cloud storage, and access controls limiting who within our organization can view your data. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Swagwise is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, please contact us immediately and we will delete it promptly.

9. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your personal information
  • Portability: Request a machine-readable copy of your data
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time
  • Restriction: Request that we limit how we use your data

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information in Section 12.

10. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete your personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of your personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your California privacy rights, contact us at the address below.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will notify you through the App or by email. Your continued use of Swagwise after any changes indicates your acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
